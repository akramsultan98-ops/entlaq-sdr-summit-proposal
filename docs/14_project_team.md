# 1. Staffing Principles

| # | Principle | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One accountable lead, one point of contact** | Entlaq should never have to work out who to call. A single Account Director owns the relationship end to end. |
| 2 | **Named ownership per venue** | Eight venue zones across three days cannot be run centrally. Each cluster has a named lead with authority on site. |
| 3 | **Decision authority on the ground** | Per [06](06_transportation_strategy.md) and [09](09_vip_experience.md), operational decisions have minutes, not hours. Escalation chains must be short. |
| 4 | **Continuity from pitch to delivery** | The people who present are the people who deliver. Handover between pitch and delivery teams is where trust is lost. |
| 5 | **Senior presence throughout the live event** | Not just at load-in. Senior guests expect senior counterparts. |
| 6 | **Protocol capability engaged early** | Ministerial liaison begins on award, not at build. Per [09](09_vip_experience.md), protocol offices work to their own timelines. |

# 5. Scaling Through the Project

| Phase | Team shape |
|---|---|
| **Award → +1 week** | Leadership team full-time. Immediate: long-lead ordering, site inspection, protocol liaison. |
| **Design & planning** | Design and technical leads scale up; suppliers contracted. |
| **Pre-production** | Full delivery team; venue leads appointed; rehearsal planning. |
| **Build** | Peak headcount. Crew across all venue clusters; overnight shifts. |
| **Live** | Full team on site. Leadership present across all three days. |
| **Load-out** | Production and logistics; leadership tapers. |
| **Post-event** | Leadership only — reconciliation, debrief, content delivery. |

# 6. Governance & Reporting

| Mechanism | Frequency | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| **Client status call** | Weekly from award; daily in the final two weeks | Account Director + Entlaq lead |
| **Written status report** | Weekly — progress, decisions required, risk register | Distributed to Entlaq |
| **Design reviews** | At defined gates | Creative Director + Entlaq |
| **Technical reviews** | At defined gates | Technical Director + Entlaq app/tech team |
| **Site meetings** | Daily during build and live | All venue leads |
| **Daily production meeting** | Each event morning | Full leadership team |
| **Post-event debrief** | Within two weeks | Both parties |

**Escalation:** Site Lead → Production Director → Account Director → Entlaq. **Any team member may escalate directly on safety.**

## Decision Summary

**Decisions Made**

| Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **Single Account Director as sole point of contact** | Entlaq should never have to determine who owns an issue |
| **Six-function leadership structure** | Maps directly to the RFP's scope-of-work sections |
| **Named site leads per venue cluster** | Eight venue zones cannot be run centrally; authority must sit on site |
| **Guest Experience elevated to director level** | The RFP names guest experience as an explicit success criterion; it is not a sub-function of production |
| **Dedicated VVIP & Protocol Lead** | Ministerial liaison is specialist work that begins on award, not at build |
| **Pitch team continues into delivery** | Handover between pitch and delivery teams is where client trust is lost |
| **Senior presence across all three live days** | Senior guests expect senior counterparts |
