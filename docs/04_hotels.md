# Hotel Evaluation — El Gouna

**Purpose:** Identify and evaluate El Gouna properties capable of supporting the ENTLAQ SDR Summit 2026 against the accommodation strategy defined in [03_accommodation.md](03_accommodation.md).

> **Status: evaluation stage. No booking decisions are being made in this document.**
> Nothing here is a final recommendation. Properties are classified as **Preferred Option**, **Alternative Option** or **Not Recommended** to show current thinking and the reasoning behind it. No inventory has been held, contacted or contracted, and no property has been approached. Final selection depends on the dependencies listed at the end of this document and on client direction.

---

## Important qualifications — read before using this document

**1. Almost everything here is External Research.** The RFP names no hotels. It states only that ~150 rooms are required *"across El Gouna hotels"* for covered guests and team. Every property, room count, facility and distance below is drawn from public sources and **requires verification directly with each property**.

**2. Distances from SÍA are estimates, not measurements.** SÍA is confirmed as an outdoor events venue operated by The Smokery, "overlooking the Red Sea in El Gouna." **Its exact address is not published** on its own channels or on El Gouna's official site. The Smokery's beach operation sits at Mangroovy Beach in the northern beach zone, which is the working assumption used here. All distances and transfer times below are therefore **estimated bands based on El Gouna's overall footprint (~10 km end to end)** and must be re-measured once SÍA's location is confirmed. Treat them as relative, not absolute.

**3. Published room counts conflict across sources.** Where sources disagree, both figures are shown. Hotel-published counts frequently include units not available to a group block.

**4. Meeting facilities are listed for context only.** The RFP places sessions at SÍA and a separate roundtable venue. Hotel meeting space is relevant here only as overflow capability, speaker prep space, or production office space — not as a programme venue.

**5. "Recommended Guest Types" indicates fit, not assignment.** The field in each hotel profile below records which categories a property *could* suit based on its characteristics. It is not an allocation. Formal classification of each property against each guest category is handled in *Evaluation by Guest Category*, using Preferred Option / Alternative Option / Not Recommended.

**6. Formatting note.** The requested per-hotel fields are preserved in full, presented as a compact table per property rather than as twelve separate headings each, which would have produced ~150 headings across the document.

### What the matrix shows

- **The luxury tier is small.** The Chedi (82), La Maison Bleue (13) and Casa Cook (~100) total under 200 rooms between them. Genuine VVIP-standard inventory in El Gouna is scarce, and the Summit is competing for it in a busy month. **This is the single most important finding in this document.**
- **Premium capacity is concentrated in three properties.** Steigenberger, Sheraton Miramar and Mövenpick hold ~1,000 rooms between them and will carry the bulk of any large block.
- **Adults-only policies constrain four properties.** Casa Cook, La Maison Bleue, Cook's Club and Fanadir cannot accommodate guests travelling with family — a real limitation for a senior audience.
- **Only two properties have verified meeting capacity.** Steigenberger (documented, ~100 theatre) and Mövenpick (largest, ~350–500). All others are unverified or absent.
- **Estimated transfer times cluster tightly.** Every property falls in an estimated 5–18 minute band. **Distance from SÍA is therefore a weak differentiator** — property character, standard and availability matter considerably more.

### Evaluation criteria applied

These are the principles behind the classifications above. They should be confirmed with the client, since a different weighting would produce different preferences.

1. **Protect the top tier absolutely.** Ministers and investors are matched to the two strongest properties. This consumes a large share of El Gouna's limited luxury inventory — which is precisely why these two categories drive everything else.
2. **Keep peer groups property-coherent.** Each category housed in a single property wherever inventory allows. Splitting a peer group across hotels creates visible inequality and avoidable coordination cost.
3. **Separate media from VVIP.** A deliberate decision, applied consistently.
4. **Separate crew from guests entirely.** Protects both guest experience and crew rest.
5. **Embed Entlaq leadership with their guests.** Hosts should sleep where their guests sleep.
6. **Hold contingency where availability pressure is lowest.**
