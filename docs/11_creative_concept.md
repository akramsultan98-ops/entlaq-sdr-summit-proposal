# Creative Concept & Design Direction

**RFP reference:** §4.1 Creative & experience design · §5.1 Creative concept and design direction

---

# 1. The Insight

Entlaq is not launching a report. It is making an ecosystem **visible**.

The Sectoral Diagnostics Report takes something that exists but cannot be seen — the real condition of ten sectors of the Egyptian economy — and renders it legible. That is what a diagnostic does. It brings the invisible into view, and once seen, it cannot be unseen. That is also precisely what turns a document into policy momentum.

The RFP's own three-act logic describes the same movement:

| Act | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Diagnose** | The whole becomes visible |
| **Dive** | The detail becomes visible |
| **Do** | The path forward becomes visible |

Every act is an act of resolution — of something blurred becoming sharp.

---

# 2. The Creative Platform

> ## MADE VISIBLE
> **From data, direction.**

The Summit is designed as a **diagnostic instrument**. Across three days it adjusts its focus three times: wide, then deep, then sharp. The guest does not simply attend three days of programming — they experience an ecosystem coming into view.

**Why this platform is right for this brief:**

- **It is native to the content.** It is drawn from what the SDR actually is, not applied to it. It would not work for another client's summit.
- **It gives the report launch a physical logic.** The data-wall is not a screen showing numbers; it is the instrument of revelation. The signature moment becomes inevitable rather than decorative.
- **It scales across every touchpoint.** Focus, resolution and depth translate to lighting, scenic, print, wayfinding, digital and stage.
- **It rhymes with the destination.** El Gouna sits on the Red Sea, where the defining experience is descending through clarifying water. The RFP's own middle act is called *Dive*. The concept and the place already agree.
- **It ends on action.** "Made visible" is not passive. Once a thing is seen, it must be answered — which is exactly the Day 3 proposition.

# 3. Translating the Three Acts

| | **Act I — DIAGNOSE** | **Act II — DIVE** | **Act III — DO** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day** | Thu 10 Sept | Fri 11 Sept | Sat 12 Sept |
| **Focus** | Wide | Deep | Sharp |
| **Visual state** | Scatter resolving to pattern | Layers, strata, depth | Convergence, single line |
| **Depth position** | Surface | Descent | Return to light |
| **Light** | Cool, dawn-blue, expansive | Deep, saturated, immersive | Warm, gold, directional |
| **Density** | Wide field, many points | Dense, layered, sectoral | Reduced, singular, resolved |
| **Sound** | Ambient, open, building | Textured, rhythmic | Clear, resolved, human |
| **Guest state** | Anticipation | Immersion | Commitment |
| **Spatial expression** | The full ecosystem in one view | Ten sectors, ten depths | One shared surface |

**The design discipline:** each day must look and feel measurably different, while unmistakably belonging to one system. A guest photographing Day 1 and Day 3 should see two distinct moments from the same event.

# 4. Visual Language

## 4.1 The Depth Gradient

The organising visual device. A vertical gradient running from **surface light** to **deep water**, mapped to the three-act journey.

Applied to: stage backdrops, print, digital, wayfinding, badge tiers, environmental graphics. It is the thread that makes eight venues read as one Summit.

## 4.2 The Sector Lattice

The SDR spans **ten priority sectors**. Ten becomes the structural number of the design system — a ten-column lattice underpinning layout, scenic rhythm, LED content and print.

At Act I the lattice is wide and even. At Act II it is interrogated, one column at a time. At Act III it converges. **The grid itself performs the narrative.**

## 4.3 Resolution as motion

The core motion principle: elements arrive **unresolved and become clear**. Scatter to order. Blur to focus. Noise to signal.

This applies to stage content, digital transitions, the data-wall and even how signage is revealed. **Nothing snaps into place; everything resolves.**

**[CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED]** — Entlaq brand typography.

Recommended structure pending confirmation: a **serif display** for statements and findings (authority, report-like gravity) paired with a **precise grotesque** for data, labels and interface (clarity, neutrality). ## 4.6 Materials & scenic

Physical language should express clarity and depth: **layered translucency** (scrim, mesh, gauze — depth you can see through), **polished and matte contrast**, **light as a material** rather than a fixture, and **restraint**. The environment should feel engineered, not dressed.

# 5. The Signature Moment — The Report Launch

**RFP §4.1:** *"an interactive main-stage reveal built around a live data-wall with audience polling."*

## 5.1 The design proposition

The report should not be *presented*. It should be **assembled in front of the audience, from the audience.**

The data-wall opens in an unresolved state — the ecosystem as noise. Across the reveal it resolves, sector by sector, into the SDR's actual findings. The live polling the RFP specifies is not an interlude; it is the mechanism. **The room's own answers are rendered into the wall in real time and set against the report's data**, so the audience sees the gap between what the ecosystem believes and what the diagnostic found.

That gap is the story. It is also the reason the room will remember it.

## 5.2 The five beats

| Beat | What happens | Guest experience |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Noise** | The wall holds unresolved data. Ambient, wide, unreadable. | "Something is forming." |
| **2 — Question** | The room is polled. Responses render live onto the wall. | "I am part of this." |
| **3 — Resolution** | Ten sectors resolve in sequence. The lattice locks. | "Now I can see it." |
| **4 — The Gap** | Room belief is set against report finding. | "That is not what we thought." |
| **5 — Consequence** | Convergence to a single statement. Handover to the human voice. | "This demands an answer." |

## 5.3 Why this design

- **It uses the app Entlaq has already built.** The RFP specifies live polling integrated with the app data feed. This design makes that integration the emotional core rather than a feature.
- **It makes the audience complicit.** A room that has contributed to a finding defends it afterwards. That is how a launch becomes momentum.
- **It is genuinely photographable.** The Gap moment is the image that travels — the single frame that carries the Summit into press and social.
- **It degrades gracefully.** If polling adoption is partial, the reveal still works on report data alone. *(Consistent with the graceful-degradation requirement in [08_registration_strategy.md](08_registration_strategy.md).)*

# 8. What This Concept Deliberately Avoids

| Avoided | Why |
|---|---|
| **Pharaonic / heritage visual tropes** | Egypt as ancient is the opposite of the message. This Summit argues for Egypt as a forward market. |
| **Generic "innovation" language** — circuits, networks, glowing nodes | Interchangeable with any tech conference on earth. |
| **Resort-destination styling** | The setting is already a resort. The design must supply the seriousness the surroundings do not. *(Per [02_destination.md](02_destination.md).)* |
| **Decorative data** | Data used as pattern undermines a diagnostic report. Every data element on display must be real. |
| **Over-production of the wellness moment** | Sunrise yoga works because it is unproduced. Restraint is the design. |

## Decision Summary

**Decisions Made**

| Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **Creative platform: "MADE VISIBLE — from data, direction"** | Native to the SDR's actual function; would not transfer to another client |
| **Three-act visual system: wide → deep → sharp** | Gives each day a distinct identity within one coherent system |
| **The Depth Gradient as the unifying device** | Makes eight dispersed venues read as one Summit |
| **The Sector Lattice built on ten** | The SDR's ten sectors become the structural grid; the grid performs the narrative |
| **"Resolution" as the universal motion principle** | Nothing snaps; everything resolves — concept expressed as behaviour |
| **The report launch designed around The Gap** | Room belief set against report finding; makes the audience complicit and produces the image that travels |
| **Amber reserved exclusively for meaning** | Protects the accent from becoming decoration |
| **Sunrise wellness deliberately unproduced** | Contrast is the design |

**Decisions Deferred**

| Deferred | Why |
|---|---|
| Final palette values and typography | Awaiting Entlaq brand guidelines |
| App visual theming | Extent of control unknown |
| Sponsor creative integration detail | Tiers TBD in the RFP |
| Scenic material specification | Requires site inspection |
| Data-wall content design | Requires SDR content and findings |
