# Production Timeline & Critical Path

**RFP reference:** §5.5 *"A production timeline and critical path from award."*

---

# 1. The Defining Constraint

The RFP sets two fixed dates:

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Proposal submission | **10 August 2026** |
| Summit delivery | **10–12 September 2026** |

**Award date is [CLIENT INPUT REQUIRED].** Assuming a typical evaluation period following the submission deadline, award lands in **mid-to-late August 2026** — leaving approximately **three to four weeks from award to first guest arrival.**

> ## This is the single most important planning fact in the project.
>
> A multi-venue destination summit for ~500 senior guests, with a large-format outdoor LED build and eight venue zones, would conventionally run a **12–16 week** production programme. We have roughly a quarter of that.

**What this changes:**

1. **Long-lead items must be ordered on the day of award.** LED, staging, structural engineering and freight cannot wait for design sign-off.
2. **Site inspection must happen within days of award**, not weeks. Seven functional spaces still have no venue per [05](05_venue_strategy.md).
3. **Design and procurement run in parallel, not in sequence.** The conventional design-then-procure model does not fit the window.
4. **Decisions become irreversible early.** After roughly Week 2, change becomes expensive or impossible.
5. **Protocol liaison starts immediately.** Ministerial offices work to their own timelines and cannot be compressed.

**We are stating this openly rather than presenting a comfortable timeline.** An agency that has not noticed the compression will discover it in Week 2.

# 2. Critical Path

The chain that determines the delivery date. **Any slip here slips the Summit.**

```
AWARD
  │
  ├─► Site inspection ────────────────────────┐
  │   (all venues, Day 3 assessment)          │
  │                                            ▼
  ├─► LED & staging order ──────────► Fabrication ──────► Freight ──────► Load-in ──► Rehearsal ──► SHOW
  │   (long-lead — day of award)                          Cairo→El Gouna
  │
  ├─► Venue confirmation ──► Technical design ──► Equipment schedule ──┘
  │   (7 spaces outstanding)
  │
  ├─► Creative sign-off ──► Content production ──► Data-wall build ──► Integration test ──┘
  │
  ├─► App data-feed spec ──► Integration development ──► Live rehearsal ──┘
  │
  └─► Protocol liaison ──► VVIP requirements ──► Venue routing ──► Delivery
```

## Critical path items

| # | Item | Why critical | Latest start |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **LED procurement & structural engineering** | Longest lead time; outdoor wind-loading engineering cannot be rushed | **Day of award** |
| 2 | **Site inspection** | Seven spaces unconfirmed; Day 3 assessment pending | **Award +3 days** |
| 3 | **Staging & scenic fabrication** | Build time plus 450 km freight | **Award +1 week** |
| 4 | **Venue confirmation** | Blocks all technical design | **Award +1 week** |
| 5 | **App data-feed integration** | Third-party dependency, outside our control | **Award +1 week** |
| 6 | **Freight call-forward from Cairo** | Single access road; no recovery if missed | **Fixed against load-in** |
| 7 | **Protocol liaison** | External timelines we cannot compress | **Day of award** |

# 4. Timeline Risks

| # | Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Award later than assumed** | Compresses an already tight programme | Confirm award date urgently; pre-agree Week 1 actions to begin on verbal award |
| 2 | **Site inspection delayed** | Blocks venue confirmation and technical design | Schedule before award if possible |
| 3 | **Seven venues remain unconfirmed** | Cannot complete technical design | Escalate at kick-off as the top priority |
| 4 | **Long-lead items ordered late** | LED and staging cannot arrive in time | Order on award; accept design risk on structure ahead of full sign-off |
| 5 | **Freight delay on the single access road** | No recovery route | Build buffer; ship earlier than required; hold critical spares on site |
| 6 | **App integration slips** | Signature moment at risk | Manual override path per [12](12_technical_production.md); test early |
| 7 | **Agenda changes late** — the RFP states timings will keep moving | Rework across every workstream | Flexible run-of-show; change cut-off at Gate 2 |
| 8 | **Day 3 assessment forces a design change** | Reopens LED and shade scope | Assess in Week 1; hold both provisions until resolved |
| 9 | **Protocol requirements arrive late** | Routing and VVIP design rework | Open liaison on day of award |
| 10 | **Supplier availability in peak season** | Equipment or crew unavailable | Contract in Week 1–2 |

## Decision Summary

**Decisions Made**

| Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **Timeline compression stated openly rather than smoothed over** | ~3–4 weeks against a conventional 12–16 week programme. An agency that hasn't noticed will discover it in Week 2. |
| **Long-lead items ordered on the day of award** | LED, staging and structural engineering cannot wait for design sign-off within this window |
| **Design and procurement run in parallel** | Sequential working does not fit the available time |
| **Four gates with a hard change cut-off at Gate 2** | Late change is the primary threat to a compressed programme |
| **Site inspection within three days of award** | Seven functional spaces have no venue; everything downstream depends on it |
| **Protocol liaison opens on day of award** | External timelines cannot be compressed |
| **Client dependencies published with dates** | Late client input compresses our programme, not theirs — visibility is the mitigation |

**Decisions Deferred**

| Deferred | Why |
|---|---|
| All absolute dates | Award date unknown |
| Load-in sequencing detail | Requires venue confirmation |
| Crew mobilisation schedule | Requires headcount and venue count |
| Rehearsal schedule | Requires agenda and speaker list |

## Open Client Questions

1. **When will the award decision be made?** This is our most time-critical question — the programme cannot be finalised without it.
2. Can the site inspection be scheduled **before** award, so Week 1 starts from a position of knowledge?
3. Is Entlaq able to authorise long-lead procurement on verbal award, ahead of contract execution?
4. What is Entlaq's internal approval cadence for creative and technical sign-off?
5. When will the agenda be locked, and what is the last acceptable date for programme changes?
6. When will SDR content and data be available for the data-wall?
7. Who authorises change after Gate 2, and what is the agreed change process?
